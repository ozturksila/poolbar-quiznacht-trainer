# Excluded Topics

Subjects the *real* Poolbar Quiznacht has already asked, reported back by
the user after quiz night. These are off-limits for every future generated
set — separate from `questions/asked-log.md`, which only tracks questions
*we've* generated. A subject can appear here even if it's never shown up
in our own sets.

Hand-maintained: append a new entry whenever the user reports a real quiz
question to avoid. Do not remove entries. Do not regenerate this file.

- **Emma Goldman** — the quote "If I can't dance, it's not my revolution"
  (her, its attribution, or trivia built around it).
- **Kurt Cobain's middle name.**
- **Martin Luther King Jr.'s "I Have a Dream" speech — where he delivered
  it** (location/venue of the speech).
- **What the acronym "GPT" stands for.**
- **The movie "The Odyssey"** (or any film adaptation of Homer's Odyssey)
  — avoid movie-trivia questions built around it.
- **Austrian families of musicians** (e.g. the Strauss family, the von
  Trapp family) as a general topic — avoid trivia centered on a famous
  Austrian musical family/dynasty.
